# SnipDeck

![SnipDeck Icon](https://github.com/Arin101230523/SnipDeck/blob/master/assets/icon.png?raw=true)

**SnipDeck** is a sleek and intuitive Chrome extension designed to streamline the way you manage both text snippets and screenshots. Whether you're a professional juggling multiple tasks or a student organizing research notes, SnipDeck is here to enhance your productivity with ease and efficiency.

## 🌟 Main Features

- **Create and Store Snippets and Screenshots**: Effortlessly create and save your frequently used text snippets and screenshots for quick access whenever you need them.
- **Manage Your Snippets and Screenshots**: Edit, delete, and search for both your text snippets and screenshots with a user-friendly interface that simplifies your workflow.
- **Dynamic Resizing**: Enjoy a seamless user experience with a dynamically resizing UI that adapts to your needs.
- **Context Menu Integration**: Quickly add selected text or capture a screenshot directly from the context menu, making the creation process smoother and more intuitive.
- **Enhanced Productivity**: Simplify the management of your text snippets and screenshots, improving your overall efficiency and streamlining your work processes.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Install SnipDeck**: Add SnipDeck to your Chrome browser by cloning this repository, going to the extensions menu, turning on developer mode, and uploading all files.
2. **Create Your First Snippet or Screenshot**: Click on the SnipDeck icon in the toolbar to open the extension and start creating your snippets or capturing screenshots.
3. **Use the Context Menu**: Right-click on the selected text or capture a screenshot and choose "Upload Selected to SnipDeck" to add it to your collection.
4. **Manage Your Snippets and Screenshots**: Open the extension popup to view, search, edit, and delete your snippets and screenshots as needed.

## 🎨 User Experience

SnipDeck is designed with a focus on user experience, featuring an intuitive UI that ensures you can manage both text snippets and screenshots without any hassle. The extension adapts to different screen sizes and resolutions, providing an optimized experience across various devices.
